COP31 COLLAPSES WITH NO DEAL

Longest summit ever ends in failure over fossil fuels.
The UN climate summit in Antalya ends with no overarching deal — the longest COP on record, the first failure since Copenhagen 2009. The EU, UK, Panama, Vanuatu, the Marshall Islands and the least-developed countries reject the “Antalya Pact” draft: it never mentions fossil fuels. The US stays away for a second year; COP32 goes to Ethiopia.
CONFIRMED · REUTERS, BBC · SUN 22 – MON 23 NOV
